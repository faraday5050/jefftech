Frequently Asked Questions

What types of AC do you service?

We service split ACs and window units for residential and commercial use — including installation, repair, maintenance, refrigerant charging and control board repair.

Do you install new AC units?

Yes. We handle new installations, replacements and relocation of AC units — set up correctly for efficiency and long-term reliability.

Do you sell ACs and refrigerators?

Yes. We supply brand-new ACs and refrigerators — including split, window and inverter ACs, single-door and double-door fridges, and chest freezers. We help you pick the right unit for your space and budget, and installation is included. Message us on WhatsApp to enquire.

Do you repair refrigerators?

Yes. We repair and maintain domestic and commercial refrigerators — including cooling faults, compressor issues, thermostat problems and electrical faults.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Minna, Niger State — and offer professional, reliable cooling system services across all thirty-six states of Nigeria.

How much does AC repair cost?

Cost depends on the fault and the parts required. We diagnose first, then tell you what's needed — no guessing, no surprises. Message us on WhatsApp with the problem and your location for a clearer picture.

How can I book a service?

Three ways: call 09135323307, message us on WhatsApp, or visit our Contact page for full contact details and locations.
